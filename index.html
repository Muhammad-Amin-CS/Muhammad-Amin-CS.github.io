Annotator disagreement and uncertainty maps for skin lesion segmentation

Sep 2026 – Oct 2026 · PyTorch, U-Net (ResNet34), IMA++ dataset (2,394 images)

When several doctors outline the same skin lesion, they rarely agree exactly. I trained a segmentation model on soft labels averaged from multiple real annotators, so its output shows where experts disagree.

  • Across three seeds, the soft-label map (AUC 0.920) beat MC-Dropout (0.856) and an edge baseline (0.871). An ensemble reached 0.929.
  • A photo-only agreement predictor ranked images by annotator disagreement better than segmentation-based uncertainty (Spearman 0.638 vs. 0.235), with a robustness check for patient and lesion overlap.

LiDAR point cloud denoising for adverse weather

Mar 2026 – Jun 2026 · Python, Open3D, NumPy, Matplotlib

Fog and rain add false points to LiDAR scans and can mislead self-driving systems. I built a modular pipeline that generates synthetic point clouds, simulates fog and rain noise, and benchmarks two classical filters: Statistical Outlier Removal and Radius Outlier Removal.

  • Compared point-cloud quality and noise-removal rates, with comparison plots and a metrics table.
  • Next steps: deep-learning denoising and physics-informed weather modeling.
